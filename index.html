The Man Behind The Mic

Comedian by Trade,
Inspiration by Choice.

Paul Lyons grew up in a house where laughter wasn't a luxury — it was the primary coping mechanism. His father, "Pop," taught him that life is mostly suffering interrupted by sandwiches, and that the suffering is funnier in retrospect.

He spent decades performing stand-up, navigating cruise ships, corporate boardrooms, and a pandemic, armed with nothing but a microphone and an unreasonable amount of optimism. His philosophy: Carpe Diem, Mañana. Seize the day. Just maybe not today.
